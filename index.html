About us · เกี่ยวกับเรา

Thai BBQ, garden-style.

Moo Kra Thoe is a mookata restaurant tucked into a quiet soi beside Udon Thani Municipal Hospital. Our tables sit under big shady trees and strings of warm lights. It's a relaxed spot to grill, chat and listen to good music after a long day.

Every table gets a charcoal mookata pan: grill the meat on the dome and let the juices run into the soup around the edge. Our sauce is a house recipe, so good that we bottle it.
